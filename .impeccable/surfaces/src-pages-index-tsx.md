# Surface brief: whole app (key, idea, requirements, generation, results)

Mode: Operate. Redesign: replaces the dark-glass / red-neon world; keeps product truth, the six-question flow, the draggable diagram.

Audience/job: founders at idea stage turning a one-line idea into an architecture and stack they can explain. Constraints: client-only, BYO OpenRouter key, no invented status or claims, plain-language glosses for jargon.

Unresolved: key persistence across reloads (stays memory-only).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The idea plotted as a flight plan on a printed en-route chart, with every component also written on a paper strip the founder can flag or strike. Refuses the dark-glass AI dashboard and the icon-card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Cool-white chart stock, navy ink, one chart blue for airways and primary action, one magenta reserved for what needs a decision. Condensed grotesque lettering (Barlow family), tabular figures, hairline rules, 2px corners. Components are drafted navaid symbols at one fixed scale, one distinct shape per type; priority is ring form (doubled, single, dashed), never hue. Strips are boxed-field paper rectangles in a rack; the legend is a ranked symbol table.

STORY: A founder sees what the tool makes, answers six plain questions, and leaves understanding each piece and why it was chosen; they can mark pieces as flagged or struck and the page honours only those marks.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, display-size condensed headline and a single idea field with its action; right, a labelled synthetic example chart drawn with the real symbols and airways, zone labels along the top edge, legend table at its foot. Primary action sits directly under the idea field. No feature-card trio, no stat row.
